A campaign starts out knowing its [experience](/docs/experience): what was measured before, by you, by your team, or by others. What you measured yourself comes from your own runs. Every finished campaign is a record of what was tried, what it scored and what happened next, and `learn()` mines that record into **cards with receipts** in your bank, so the next campaign that loads the bank starts knowing what the last one measured.

Each lesson is a card with a credence score (the card's reliability score), boundary conditions on where it applies (its scope and the conditions in its body) and the evidence behind every update, so the next build's ideation and implementation can decide how far to rely on it rather than simply retrieving it.

```bash
kapso learn ./campaign
kapso learn ./campaign --harness ./harnesses/trading     # when the campaign's record names none
```

```python
lesson = kapso.learn(solution)
print(lesson.explain())
```

## Why measured experience

A page tells you what people have written down. It cannot tell you which of those approaches paid off on *your* task, at *your* scale, against *your* evaluation. The bank answers that: each card records a mechanism that paid or failed, the conditions it held under and the measurements behind it. A card is never a bare assertion. It carries a reliability state, a score and the evidence trail that earned them, and a score with no written rationale fails conformance (`reliability rationale missing — no naked scores`).

## What a card is

A card has a **body**, the fact itself, written to be read cold by an engineer who has never seen it, whose first heading states the rule as a plain sentence and whose `**Rule:**` line is the card's *hero*, the one-liner the bank index shows; and a **reliability block**, what the evidence says about it.

| Card type | What it holds |
|-----------|---------------|
| `insight` | A mechanism that paid or failed, and the conditions under which it holds |
| `procedure` | A runnable recipe. Its code ships with the card, under `procedures/<name>/` |

A card carries its `scope`: `domain`, or the `harness:<name>` coordinate it was measured under (`harness:none` for a campaign that ran without one). The coordinate is read off the campaign's record, never invented. Its evidence entries say what a campaign did with the claim, one verdict each (`confirm`, `weaken`, `refine`, `refute`, `spawn`, `exercise`); a card converted from a curated source carries a `reported` entry instead, the score others reported with its author, licence and protocol, and is served as such, never as a measurement of yours.

A card sits in one of five reliability states, and only two reach a campaign:

| State | Serves? | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `candidate` | yes | New, or a generalizing successor. Its claim is a prediction with its test attached |
| `active` | yes | Carrying evidence that supports it |
| `cold` | no | Kept, but not served |
| `retired` | no | Withdrawn. Frozen history under `retired/`, never modified again |
| `superseded` | no | Replaced by a successor that generalizes it; the two link both ways |

A card may declare that it `contradicts` another active card. When two such cards are served together, the pair is named to the reading agent rather than presented side by side as though they agreed.

## How a campaign becomes a card

One finished campaign moves through four stages. Each is bounded by a **frame** that does the mechanical work and validates the result; the judgment inside is done by agent sessions the frame launches, staged with the harness's learning module when the campaign ran under one.

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| Import | The campaign enters the trajectory store under a stable identity, with the experience it was served and the harness it ran under (the campaign-side save that feeds it is the harvest) |
| Mine | The bundle is read into derived `mined/` views: strategy, operations, artifacts |
| Grade | The bank is graded against the campaign *before* it learns from it, so the score means something |
| Update | The update crew folds the result in as one reviewed commit, tagged `lr_<id>`, pushed to your share when the bank has one |

Grading before learning is the `exam` argument of [`learn()`](/docs/reference/kapso-api#learn); it defaults to `True`, and `False` is for development replays only. The stages, the graders and the learner's own development are on [Development mode](/docs/learn/development-mode).

## What a learn run does

After each run, `learn()`:

1. updates the credence and the boundaries of the lessons the run used, from their effect on the result (the evidence ledger's verdicts: `confirm`, `weaken`, `refine`, `refute`);
2. induces lessons from similar traces, or deduces one from a single piece of evidence and its mechanism;
3. merges lessons that overlap, the successor linked to what it generalises;
4. A/B tests the updated lessons by running the same build with and without them, so an update is measured rather than guessed ([Development mode](/docs/learn/development-mode) has the arms and their verdict).

## Where the bank lives

```bash
kapso experience add mine                                          # creates it; a first learn() does too
kapso experience share https://github.com/your-org/kapso-bank.git  # every learn() pushes there from then on
kapso experience list                                              # the head, the card count, where it shares to
```

The bank is a git repository at `learning.bank.local_path` (shipped as `~/.kapso/bank.git`): one bank per user, outside any project. Each card is a file, each lesson a tagged commit, so a campaign can be pinned to an exact bank state. Credentials stay with `git` and `gh`; Kapso never handles them. Sharing, reading a team's bank and the other sources are on [Your bank](/docs/experience/use) and [Share a bank](/docs/experience/share).

## How the next campaign reads it

A campaign reads a bank exactly when it loads it: `kapso build --experience mine`, the brief's `Experience` line, or `load_experience("mine")`. There is no serving switch; a build that loads nothing is served nothing. `mine` reads your bank's home and a bank you read is served from its clone in the store, so no campaign step touches the network once the clone exists: the first load of a bank's URL clones it into the store, and `kapso experience update <url>` refreshes the clone, which is how to put it in place before a launch with no network.

Serving is an introduction, not an injection. At launch the frame clones each bank at a **pinned head** (a resume re-pins the same head even when the bank moved since), compiles an introduction with one line per source and its provenance, and stages three tools under `.kapso/serving/` in the campaign; the agent decides what to open.

| Tool | Returns | Call it when |
|------|---------|--------------|
| `bank_index(section=)` | The whole eligible bank as one index page: name, one-liner, score, applies-when | Your next decision might have been faced before. Cheap |
| `bank_get_card(cards, source=)` | Full card bodies; a procedure's code path with it (`source` picks the bank when more than one is loaded) | You want the advice |
| `bank_get_card_with_evidence(cards, source=)` | The card plus its reliability block and evidence trail | Before you stake real budget on it |

The index is the whole eligible set in plain reliability order, with no `k` cap and no relevance cut. Two things are filtered, and the frame says which: **scope** (under a [harness](/docs/harnesses), `mine` serves the cards scoped `domain` or `harness:<name>`; a card outside is refused by name with the reason; `mine:all` serves every card you measured; a bank you read serves everything it holds) and **quarantine**, silently (decoys and the non-serving states). A card may carry a measurement offer, a *probe*. It rides card reads only, never the index or the introduction; it is optional and never the default gate: the agent adopts its protocol only where that is affordable at the data's scale, and says so, and an ignored probe stays queued; at most `learning.retriever.probe_budget` offers ride a campaign (shipped at `1`), and offers ride `mine` alone, since a bank you read is not yours to measure for.

Every bank read is logged. The pull log is the bank tools' record: which cards the agent opened, at what depth and from which bank, which is what grading scores serving from and what credits a cited lesson to the bank it was read from (a hosted gate's or an index's reads are in the session's own record).
