# Evaluation Profile

The immutable grader runs `main.py` in an isolated subprocess, requires integer `(27, 10)` validation predictions, scores all 27 validation rows with the official RelBench implementation, and archives full-fidelity predictions. The registered fraction and seed do not subsample scoring rows. The primary score is validation `link_prediction_map`; precision and recall are reported alongside it, while test labels are unavailable.

The task has 2,649 training rows at 54 annual timestamps from 1951 through 2004, with 23–108 rows per timestamp and 765 distinct drivers. Training target sizes have mean 6.875, median 4, maximum 18, and 34.5% contain at least ten circuits. The eleven modern selection seasons 1994–2004 range from 23 to 46 rows per season and have much larger target lists than the full historical distribution. Validation and test each contain 27 distinct drivers at one timestamp; validation target sizes have mean 13.926, median 18, maximum 19, and 74.1% contain at least ten circuits.

Coverage axes are era/season, full versus partial attendance, career recency, driver experience, circuit hosting persistence and gaps, normalized expected round, geography, constructor viability, and driver–circuit/constructor–circuit history. MAP@10 normalizes by `min(target_count, 10)`, so calendar ordering dominates full-season rows while attendance ordering has the greatest leverage on partial rows. The supplied 80.1% modern full-season claim is consistent with the 1994–2004 profile but not with the full 1951–2004 training mix, where only 34.5% have at least ten labels; training therefore uses eight-year recency weights with a 0.05 floor and selection is restricted to 1994–2004.

The protected `kapso_evaluation/` directory was not modified; this profile is stored at repository root to preserve evaluator integrity.

The cached critical-path pair artifact contains 203,973 Model A rows and 213,752 Model B rows with 92 features. Its one-time build measured 208–230 seconds; cache reload measured under one second. Full three-seed fold throughput measured 21–29 seconds per season, comfortably exceeding the pre-committed one-fold-per-90-seconds target. Per-season and full/partial count/MAP strata are persisted in each run's `metrics.json`.
