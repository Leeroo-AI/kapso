# Evaluation measurement profile

## Mechanics

The immutable grader runs `main.py` in an isolated subprocess, scores every one of the 960 validation rows with RelBench's official metrics, and archives only full-fidelity runs. The score of record is validation ROC AUC. Fast fidelity changes candidate build mode but does not sample validation rows. Required prediction vectors are float probabilities aligned to the original validation and test row order, with shapes `(960,)` and `(825,)`.

The validation vector must be generated by a chain fit without validation labels. The test vector may use a frozen design refit on train plus validation after the validation vector has been produced. All feature rows linked through dated relations are restricted to dates at or before the seed origin.

## Input distribution

- Train has 11,994 rows, 11,994 distinct trials, and annual origins from 2001-01-05 through 2019-01-01. Counts grow from 2 in 2001 to roughly 1,100 per recent origin. No trial repeats within train.
- Validation has 960 distinct trials at the single origin 2020-01-01 and positive rate 0.584375. Test has 825 distinct trials at 2021-01-01. Trial IDs do not overlap across splits.
- Train positive rate is 0.637569. The label-generation population contains at least one future qualifying analysis by construction. Qualifying future analysis count has train median 1, p90 5, p99 24, maximum 703, and mean 2.867; validation median 1, p90 5, p99 about 32, maximum 116, and mean 2.926.
- Every train, validation, and test seed has zero qualifying own analyses, outcomes, withdrawals, and reported-event totals at or before its origin. This contradicts the solution's cold-start uncertainty: cold start is measured at 100%, so those tables contribute through future multiplicity targets for labeled rows and leak-free cohort/neighborhood state, not seed ego-history.
- As-of zero-coverage shares for relation rows are: condition 9.24% train / 10.63% validation / 9.33% test; intervention 37.31% / 53.44% / 49.70%; sponsor 0% in all splits; facility 8.52% / 1.35% / 1.45%. Design and eligibility coverage is effectively complete.
- Complete criteria coverage is 100%; median character length is 1,268 train, 1,510 validation, and 1,736 test. Brief summaries have 100% coverage and median lengths 336, 370, and 346. Detailed descriptions cover 62.12%, 67.29%, and 63.52%, with train p99 7,954 characters. Titles are essentially complete.
- Median trial age rises from 675 days in train to 790.5 in validation and 872 in test. Intervention and facility coverage drift materially, while text length and detailed-description coverage drift moderately.
- Official training already contains annual origins. Replay must use genuinely absent origins such as July cutoffs and is deferred until the banked candidate exists.

## Coverage axes

Origin year; phase and source class; trial age; enrollment scale; design and masking; intervention availability; condition breadth; sponsor identity/class; site and country breadth; text presence and length; eligibility complexity; multiplicity bin; cohort rarity/novelty; and history segment. The own-history depth segment is degenerate at zero, so slice reporting emphasizes trial age, multiplicity proxy, relation coverage, text richness, phase, and enrollment.

## Resolution and representativeness checks

Candidate prediction rank correlations, clustered bootstrap standard errors, and slice AUCs are computed only after the design and validation vector are frozen. They are diagnostics and never feed model, prompt, feature, round, or ensemble selection. Representativeness comparisons use origin volume, known train/validation label rates, and unlabeled test feature distributions.

This profile is stored outside `kapso_evaluation/` because the supplied evaluation directory is explicitly read-and-execute-only; editing any file beneath it is defined as evaluation tampering.
