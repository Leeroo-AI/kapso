# Evaluation Profile

## Mechanics

- Registered command: `python kapso_evaluation/kapso_eval.py --fidelity full --fraction 1.0 --seed 1337`.
- The immutable grader runs `main.py` in an isolated subprocess, validates finite prediction files, scores the complete 71,474-row validation table through the official RelBench evaluator, and archives full-fidelity outputs.
- The primary selection metric is validation accuracy. The harness also reports macro F1, micro F1, and MRR. `fraction` and `seed` do not subsample rows.
- Validation predictions must be generated by the train-only Model A. Model B may use train and validation labels only for test predictions.

## Input Distribution

- Train: 340,491 rows from 2018-01-02 through 2020-01-31, with 1,224 nonzero labels across 29 nonzero classes.
- Validation: 71,474 rows from 2020-02-01 through 2020-06-30, with 67 nonzero labels across classes 3, 6, 10, 14, 15, 23, and 25. The class-zero baseline is 71,407/71,474 = 0.999062596.
- Test: 88,942 rows from 2020-07-01 through 2020-12-31. Labels are unavailable.
- All 500,907 task seeds join one-to-one to a header at the identical timestamp and have at least one same-document item. No item timestamp is after its document seed time.
- Train/validation/test contain 38/34/40 observed exact sales areas and 30/29/34 sales organizations. Test expands beyond training in header-category coverage.
- Median item count is 2/1/1 for train/validation/test; the respective 99th percentiles are 54/48/57. Median distinct-product count is 1 in every split.
- All four primary item party roles are equal for 57.55%/57.50%/58.11% of train/validation/test documents. Fewer than 0.1% of documents vary a role within the document.

## Coverage Axes

- Temporal origin and five-month forward window.
- Zero versus nonzero target prevalence and nonzero office identity.
- Exact sales area, document type, billing company, currency, and time-of-creation cohort.
- Item-volume, product/category diversity, product-history support, and unseen-product backoff.
- Sold-to, ship-to, bill-to, and payer identity, role agreement, and customer-by-area activity.
- Country/region geography reached through each party's customer and address records.
- History count, purity, recency, and temporal-decay support at each frozen deployment cutoff.

## Critical Path

The limiting artifact is the internally selected, temporally frozen binary hurdle plus conditional label histogram decoder. Database loading and full item aggregation measured 7.6 seconds total on the assigned lane, so repeated LightGBM origin fits and their causal history matrices, rather than relational joining, bound the achievable experiment rate.
