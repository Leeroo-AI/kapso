# Insights

- [censored-event-history-is-the-survival-spine](censored-event-history-is-the-survival-spine.md) — Across two campaigns on two fixed-horizon no-event tasks, rich point-in-time event, cohort, and counterpart history dominated the survival-head choice.
- [compact-tabular-beats-sequence-heads](compact-tabular-beats-sequence-heads.md) — In late-regime or distribution-shifted churn, a viable event sequence does not make its neural head competitive with compact cutoff-safe summaries.
- [direct-binary-companions-stabilize-survival](direct-binary-companions-stabilize-survival.md) — In the single campaign measured, a simple direct horizon companion matched or beat elaborate meta-learning and shift weighting; its two lanes are only weakly independent.
- [focused-cutoff-safe-relational-summaries](focused-cutoff-safe-relational-summaries.md) — Small grouped summaries aligned to renewal and neighborhood mechanisms outperform indiscriminate relational column widening.
- [graph-rejections-are-variant-bounded](graph-rejections-are-variant-bounded.md) — A zero-weight graph member falsifies its implemented recipe, not graph variants whose information path or objective was never tested.
- [static-audit-flags-require-contract-validation](static-audit-flags-require-contract-validation.md) — Pattern-based publishing flags are review hooks; binding validity comes from traced dataflow and isolated dynamic evaluation.
- [sub-noise-orderings-are-ties](sub-noise-orderings-are-ties.md) — When paired score differences are smaller than their uncertainty, the artifact order is bookkeeping, not evidence of a better model.
- [training-reweighting-does-not-replace-representation](training-reweighting-does-not-replace-representation.md) — Under temporal drift, changing sample weights or loss geometry without changing the representation tends to fail forward gates or yield noise-sized gains.
