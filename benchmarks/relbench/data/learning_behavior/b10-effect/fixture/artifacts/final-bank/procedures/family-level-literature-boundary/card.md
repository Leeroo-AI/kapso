Method [E1][E2][E3]: before browsing, write an allowlist of family-level mechanisms, analogous domains, and general implementation references, plus a blocklist of the task's leaderboard, task paper tables, tuned configurations, and solution code. Phrase queries at the family and mechanism level, log opened sources, and reject any source whose usefulness depends on knowing the target task's result. Carry only mechanism and generic engineering constraints into ideation; task-specific facts must come from the campaign's own permitted data and measurements. Audit the source log against the boundary before candidates are selected.
