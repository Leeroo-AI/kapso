Method [E1][E2][E3][E4]: before each expensive phase, name the unresolved decision and the result that would change it; if neither sign can change the action, stop the phase. Use one cheap debug gate to establish wiring, freeze a rejected component as soon as its weight or keep decision is fixed, and permit one full integrity confirmation at the irreversible final handoff. Persist row-aligned out-of-fold predictions, legal caches, rejection records, and a valid fallback before branching so later analysis does not repay the measurement. Protect the finalization reserve as an interior deadline, spending saved budget on a structurally different candidate rather than another same-lineage tie.
